# Step by step

Each page here does **one task**, in numbered steps. The steps are tick boxes: tick them as you
go, and the page remembers where you were (in this browser only).

| Task | Time | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Replace one sound and install the mod | 10 min | [Make your first mod](first-mod.md) |
| Change how a sound plays: volume, distance, loop | 5 min | [Tune a sound](tune-a-sound.md) |
| Reuse a selection of sounds, yours or the community's | 2 min | [Use a preset](use-a-preset.md) |
| Work on one mod with other people through GitHub | 15 min | [Collaborate on a mod](collaborate.md) |
| Get the latest version of BSM | 1 min | [Update the app](update-the-app.md) |

**Before you start:** BSM is [installed](../getting-started/install.md) and your library is
[scanned](../getting-started/first-launch.md#4-scan-the-library).

For what every button on a screen does, see the [Features](../features/library.md) pages.
